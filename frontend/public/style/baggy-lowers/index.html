Fit, before size

Baggy is a cut.
Not a guess.

Measure a pair you already like.

Lay it flat and note the waist, rise, thigh and inseam. Follow each retailer’s measuring method; a body measurement and a garment measurement are not interchangeable.

Choose the silhouette deliberately.

A wide leg gives room down the leg; a barrel shape adds a more curved outline. “Relaxed” and “oversized” vary by brand, so use the size chart rather than the label alone.

Check length with your footwear.

Choose the amount of fabric break you want over your shoes. Too much length can drag; an alteration may change the original shape.

Read the sale and return terms.

Check the selected size, delivery charge and exchange eligibility at checkout. Do not assume sale items have the same policy as full-price items.
