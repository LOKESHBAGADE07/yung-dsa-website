1. Establish the appearance.

Find the original post or authorised source. Record its date and context without inventing an episode number.

2. Identify the actual product.

Look for a clear item credit or confirmation from the artist, stylist or brand that links that appearance to a product.

3. Keep alternatives separate.

Similar shape or colour is useful for shopping, but it does not prove a match. Alternatives keep the “Similar style” label.
