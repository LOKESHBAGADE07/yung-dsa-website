Yung DSA hoodies.
Know the source.

The brand, the hoodie and the price are three different questions. Here is what the available sources establish before you shop.

By Lokesh Bagade · Reviewed 26 November 2026

The short answer

We have not verified a retail clothing brand owned by Yung DSA. A BAPE hoodie identification circulating among fans remains an unconfirmed outfit lead. The ₹92,000 figure is not a verified purchase price.

Does Yung DSA have a brand or company?

Searches for “Yung DSA company” can refer to a record label, a clothing credit, a crew name or an artist-owned business. These are not interchangeable. A brand appearing in a video does not establish who owns it, and a music credit does not make that record label the artist’s clothing company.

There is a useful primary-source clothing credit: the official Yeda Yung video names Urban Monkey and Bank of Brokes as clothing partners and Gully Gang Records as the label. That supports a partnership credit for that video. It does not identify every outfit in the video, establish an ongoing endorsement, or confirm any Bigg Boss hoodie. Read the official video credits.

We have not found reliable confirmation of a retail clothing label owned by him in the sources linked here. That is an evidence limit, not a claim that no business can exist. For a supposed official store, start from a link published on the artist’s own channels rather than a seller using his name.

Was the Bigg Boss hoodie a BAPE shark hoodie?

A fan discussion on Reddit connects a kitchen incident to an expensive hoodie and offers an identification. It is a lead, not confirmation from the artist, stylist or brand. We have not independently confirmed the exact episode item, colourway, authenticity or a ₹92,000 valuation.

BAPE does sell shark full-zip hoodies. Its official STA Camo product page describes a full zip, camouflage pattern and shark motif. That explains the design family; it does not prove the television garment is that product. Different releases, markets, taxes and resale sellers can also produce very different prices.

Choose the silhouette before the logo

For a similar mood, decide whether you want a clean dark zip hoodie or a graphic camouflage layer. A full-length zip changes how the front hangs when open; a pull-over keeps a more continuous front shape. Check the actual garment measurements rather than buying an extra-large size automatically.

  • Chest: compare a well-fitting hoodie laid flat with the retailer’s measuring method.
  • Length: compare the hem with the rise of your trousers; a very long top can hide the shape of a wide leg.
  • Shoulders and sleeves: a dropped shoulder creates room differently from simply increasing every measurement.
  • Fabric: check composition and lining. Heavy fleece and a lighter knit will feel different in Indian weather.

Before paying, confirm the exact variant, delivered price, size exchange eligibility and return terms. An unusually low “same as celebrity” listing is not proof of a genuine branded product. This guide does not link to replicas or take payment.
