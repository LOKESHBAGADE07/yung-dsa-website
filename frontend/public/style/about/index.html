About this guide

About the style guide.

Edited by Lokesh Bagade · Checked 13 Nov 2026

Who made this?

This independent fan project is created and edited by Lokesh Bagade. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Yung DSA, his team, the show, or the listed retailers. Artist bookings and business enquiries are not accepted here.

How products are selected

The edit compares streetwear silhouettes, listed prices and useful fit details from retailer pages. All current products are similar-style options. Product listings establish the retailer’s description, not that the artist wore the item.

Original generic illustrations help distinguish categories. They are not product photos. Use the retailer link to inspect actual photos, fabric information, measurements and current availability.

Evidence labels

  • Similar style: an editorial alternative; no artist-worn claim.
  • Unverified brand lead: a source worth checking; no confirmed product identity.
  • Exact match: reserved for a specific product supported by a primary source. No current listing has this label.

Prices, links and commission

Listed prices were checked on 13 Nov 2026. They are a snapshot, not a live feed or a promise of stock. Retailers may change prices, sizes, delivery charges and policies.

All current retailer links are direct, non-affiliate links. This website does not earn commission from them. No paid product placement is included. If approved affiliate links or sponsored placements are added later, the relevant pages and links will be labelled.

Purchases and order support

Checkout happens on the retailer’s website. We do not collect payment, place orders, ship products or handle returns. Confirm all purchase details with that retailer before paying.

Privacy on these pages

The filters work locally in your browser without an account. These pages use the site’s Vercel page analytics integration when available, and skip loading it when the browser signals Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. They do not collect order details or payment information. External retailers have their own privacy policies.

Corrections and review

Product sources are linked directly from each card. The look journal links its evidence separately and marks unresolved claims. If you notice a mistake, you can contact the editor through Lokesh’s LinkedIn profile.
