Artist Biography

YUNG DSA (Harsh Vijay Machare) is an Indian hip-hop artist and rapper from Yerwada, Pune 06. The links below connect selected releases to their original video sources.

From early viral drops like YUNG SITAR (2021) and STRAIGHT OUTTA YERWADA (2023) to his breakout single YEDA YUNG on Gully Gang Records (2024) and major record label release MAAF KAR on Sony Music India (2025), the selection below spans several stages of his music catalogue.
