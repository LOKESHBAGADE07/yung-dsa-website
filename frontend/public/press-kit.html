Artist Biography

Real name
Harsh Vijay Machare
Hometown
Yerwada, Pune
Breakthrough song
Yeda Yung (2024)

Before he made music full-time, Yung DSA worked at a BPO. He was already putting his neighbourhood on record with tracks like Straight Outta Yerwada (2023). In a May 2025 interview with Rolling Stone India, he said the success of Yeda Yung let him leave his job. He wrote Maaf Kar in the months that followed to reflect on his past and the people who had stood by him.

In September 2026 he appeared on Bigg Boss 20. The Times of India profile traces his path from Yerwada to the show. Find the broadcaster's welcome video and dated episode sources in the Bigg Boss 20 guide.
