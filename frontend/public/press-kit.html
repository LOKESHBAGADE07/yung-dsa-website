Artist Biography

YUNG DSA (Harsh Vijay Machare) is an Indian hip-hop artist and rapper from Yerwada, Pune 06. Known for his distinct street storytelling, gritty flow, and energetic delivery, he represents the raw pulse of Indian underground hip-hop transitioned into mainstream acclaim.

From early viral drops like YUNG SITAR (2021) and STRAIGHT OUTTA YERWADA (2023) to his breakout single YEDA YUNG on Gully Gang Records (2024) and major record label release MAAF KAR on Sony Music India (2025), YUNG DSA has solidified his position as one of Pune's premier hip-hop exports.
