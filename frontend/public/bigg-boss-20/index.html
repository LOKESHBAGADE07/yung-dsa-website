Who is Yung DSA?

The Times of India’s artist profile identifies Yung DSA as Harsh Vijay Machare from Yerwada, Pune. Before music became his full-time job, he worked at a BPO to support his family. In a May 2025 interview with Rolling Stone India, he explained that the success of Yeda Yung let him leave that job.

To hear the music that preceded his television appearance, start with Yeda Yung and Maaf Kar. The artist biography collects more release credits and interviews.
